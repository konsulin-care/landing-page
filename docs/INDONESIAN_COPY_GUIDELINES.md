# Indonesian Copy Guidelines

This document provides guidelines for writing copy that resonates with Indonesian users in the mental health context. It covers tone, phrasing, terminology, and cultural considerations.

## Core Principles

### 1. **Conversational, Not Clinical**

Mental health is stigmatized in Indonesia. Clinical or formal language triggers defensiveness.

**Examples**:

| ❌ Clinical/Formal | ✓ Conversational |
|---|---|
| "Manfaatkan alat skrining kesehatan mental yang berbasis bukti" | "Coba test kesehatan mental, tau hasilnya langsung" |
| "Implementasikan protokol jurnal harian untuk mengelola distres" | "Curah hati di Konsulin, lihat polanya sendiri" |
| "Konsultasikan dengan profesional kesehatan mental tersertifikasi" | "Chat sama psikolog, kapan kamu butuh" |
| "Optimalisasi kesejahteraan psikologis melalui intervensi digital" | "Pahami diri sendiri dengan bantuan Konsulin" |

**Rule**: Use language someone would use texting a friend, not presenting to a board.

---

### 2. **Direct Address (Kamu, Bukan "Pengguna")**

Indonesian audiences respond to direct, personal language.

**Examples**:

| ❌ Impersonal | ✓ Direct |
|---|---|
| "Pengguna dapat mencatat pikiran mereka" | "Kamu bisa curah hati di sini, tanpa ada yang lihat" |
| "Individu mengalami gejala depresi" | "Kamu merasa tertekan? Ini normal, dan kita bisa bantu" |
| "Pelanggan akan menerima notifikasi" | "Kamu bakal dapet reminder setiap hari" |

**Rule**: Use "kamu" or "lu" (for younger audiences). Avoid "pengguna" or "individu."

---

### 3. **Problem-First, Not Feature-First**

Name the pain before offering the solution.

**Examples**:

| ❌ Feature-First | ✓ Problem-First |
|---|---|
| "Konsulin menyediakan jurnal digital" | "Lagi kepingin curhat tapi takut diketahui orang?" |
| "Platform kami memiliki skrining psikometrik" | "Pengen tau kesehatan mental kamu, tapi malu pergi ke dokter?" |
| "Fitur appointment management tersedia" | "Sering lupa jadwal visit ke psikolog?" |

**Rule**: Always ask "What problem does this solve?" before introducing the feature.

---

### 4. **Acknowledge Stigma Directly**

Indonesian users worry about judgment. Address this fear head-on.

**Examples**:

| ❌ Ignoring Stigma | ✓ Addressing Stigma |
|---|---|
| "Dapatkan akses ke layanan kesehatan mental" | "Akses kesehatan mental, tanpa perlu takut dijurin tetangga" |
| "Jurnal digital untuk tracking mood" | "Tulis jurnal pribadi kamu, cuma kamu yang lihat" |
| "Konsultasi dengan psikolog terpercaya" | "Chat sama psikolog, semuanya rahasia" |

**Rule**: If your solution removes a specific barrier (privacy, judgment, cost), say it explicitly.

---

### 5. **Emphasize Privacy and Safety**

Indonesians are cautious about data and judgment. Make privacy non-negotiable in copy.

**Examples**:

| ❌ Taking Privacy for Granted | ✓ Asserting Privacy |
|---|---|
| "Platform kami berbasis cloud" | "Data kamu dienkripsi, hanya kamu yang bisa akses" |
| "Jurnal tersimpan secara digital" | "Jurnal kamu pribadi. Kami enggak baca, enggak jual data" |
| "Konsultasi tercatat di sistem" | "Chat sama psikolog, percakapan kamu aman dan rahasia" |

**Rule**: Don't assume users trust tech. Show them why they can trust you.

## Indonesian Mental Health Terminology

### Common Terms (Use These)

| English | Indonesian (Common) | Context |
|---------|---|---|
| Mental health | Kesehatan mental | General umbrella term |
| Mental health check-in | Skrining kesehatan mental, test mental | Assessment/self-check |
| Mood tracking | Tracking mood | Daily logging |
| Journaling | Jurnal, curah hati | Writing practice |
| Stress | Stress | Work/life pressure |
| Anxiety | Kecemasan, kekhawatiran | Fear/worry |
| Depression | Depresi | Persistent sadness |
| Burnout | Burnout | Exhaustion from work |
| Counseling | Konseling, konsultasi | Professional guidance |
| Psychologist | Psikolog | Mental health professional |
| Digital intervention | Intervensi digital, dukungan digital | Tech-based support |
| Wellness | Wellness, kesejahteraan | General well-being |

### Avoid These Terms (They Feel Clinical)

| Term | Why Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Gangguan mental" (mental disorder) | Sounds pathologizing | Kesehatan mental, kondisi mental |
| "Pasien" (patient) | Too clinical, othering | Pengguna, kamu |
| "Intervensi terapeutik" | Too formal | Dukungan, bantuan |
| "Patologi psikis" | Too academic | Masalah kesehatan mental |
| "Terapi kognitif-perilaku" (CBT) | Jargon | Teknik pikiran-perilaku, cara mengelola pikiran |

## Mental Health Context in Indonesia

### Key Fears/Barriers (Address These in Copy)

**1. Stigma from Family/Community**
- Many Indonesians fear judgment from orang tua, saudara, or neighbors
- Solution framing: "Pribadi" (private), "Tanpa perlu bilang siapa-siapa" (don't tell anyone)

**2. Distrust of Psychology**
- Some believe mental health issues are "spiritual" or "moral failures"
- Solution framing: "Berbasis sains" (science-backed), "Diakui dokter" (doctor-approved), reference research

**3. Cost Concerns**
- Therapy is expensive; not covered by BPJS for many
- Solution framing: "Tanpa biaya" (free), "Lebih murah dari terapis" (cheaper than therapy), "Mulai dari kecil" (start small)

**4. Skepticism About Digital Solutions**
- "Can an app really help with mental health?"
- Solution framing: "Ribuan sudah buktiin" (thousands have tried), "Test ilmiah yang terbukti" (proven scientific tests), testimonials from real people

**5. Time Pressure**
- Busy schedules, no time to "go to therapy"
- Solution framing: "Kapan saja" (anytime), "5 menit saja" (just 5 minutes), "Di rumah aja" (from home)

## Tone Variations by Audience Segment

### **For Young Adults (18-25, Self-Care Focused)**
- **Tone**: Casual, relatable, peer-to-peer
- **Triggers**: FOMO, self-discovery, relatability to influencers/peers
- **Examples**:
  - "Tau enggak? Mood tracking bisa bantu kamu jadi lebih kenal diri sendiri"
  - "Gampang aja, langsung chat, langsung dapet hasil"
  - "Banyak yang udah mulai, kamu kapan?"

### **For Professionals (25-40, Productivity/Wellbeing Focused)**
- **Tone**: Practical, results-oriented, time-saving
- **Triggers**: Efficiency, evidence, ROI on wellness
- **Examples**:
  - "Kelola stress lebih smart: insights harian tentang mood kamu"
  - "5 menit jurnal bisa save 1 jam panic later"
  - "Riset menunjukkan tracking mood tingkatkan produktivitas"

### **For Parents (40+, Parenting/Family Health Focused)**
- **Tone**: Caring, protective, family-oriented
- **Triggers**: Child well-being, family harmony, "doing the right thing"
- **Examples**:
  - "Bantu anak kamu pahami perasaannya lebih baik"
  - "Kesehatan mental keluarga dimulai dari diri sendiri"
  - "Sudah saatnya kita bicara soal kesehatan mental"

## Phrasing Patterns (Indonesian-Specific)

### **Starting Statements (Hook)**

Effective patterns for problem statements:

- "Lagi [emotion/situation]?" → "Lagi kepingin curhat? Atau mau tau kesehatan mental kamu?"
- "Siapa yang [solves problem]?" → "Siapa yang mendengarkan perasaanmu?"
- "Pengen [aspirational outcome]?" → "Pengen tau kenapa mood kamu naik-turun?"
- "Takut [fear]?" → "Takut dikira gila kalo curhat soal kesehatan mental?"

---

### **Outcome Statements (Benefit)**

Effective patterns for what users will experience:

- "Kamu bisa [action], [benefit]" → "Kamu bisa curah hati, lihat polanya sendiri"
- "[Action] tanpa [barrier]" → "Chat sama psikolog, tanpa perlu takut diketahui"
- "[Outcome], [timeframe]" → "Pahami diri sendiri, dalam hitungan hari"
- "Dari [problem] jadi [solution]" → "Dari tertekan random jadi paham penyebabnya"

---

### **Trust Builders (Proof/Credibility)**

Effective patterns for building credibility:

- "Berbasis [credibility]" → "Berbasis riset psikolog Indonesia"
- "[Number] sudah [social proof]" → "Ribuan sudah mulai, kamu kapan?"
- "Sudah [achievement]" → "Sudah dipercaya oleh [organization]"
- "[Expert] rekomen" → "Psikolog rekomen jurnal pagi"

---

### **CTA Statements (Action)**

Effective patterns for calls-to-action:

- "[Action] aja" → "Coba aja, gratis kok"
- "[Action] sekarang" → "Mulai sekarang, dari WhatsApp"
- "Jangan [negative]" → "Jangan tunggu, kondisinya makin parah"
- "[Action] [ease]" → "Chat, gampang, langsung dapet hasil"

## Copy Examples by Section

### **Hero Section**

```
Headline: "Kesehatan mental kamu penting. Tapi siapa yang mendengarkan?"
Subheading: "Konsulin adalah kawan digital untuk memahami diri sendiri—kapan saja, 
tanpa harus malu, tanpa biaya."
CTA: "Mulai di WhatsApp"
```

**Why it works**:
- Problem-first: Addresses isolation + lack of support
- Direct: "Kamu" implied
- Removes barriers: "tanpa harus malu, tanpa biaya"
- Action-oriented: "Mulai sekarang"

---

### **Journaling Value Block**

```
Problem: "Lagi kepingin curhat tapi takut diketahui?"
Outcome: "Tulis perasaanmu, cuma kamu yang lihat. Lama-lama kamu lihat polanya sendiri."
Proof (on expand): "Research menunjukkan journaling kurangi anxiety 30%."
CTA: "Coba sekarang"
```

**Why it works**:
- Addresses specific fear: Judgment/privacy
- Direct benefit: "Lihat polanya" (understand yourself)
- Evidence: Research mention (not overwhelming)
- Easy action: "Coba sekarang" (low barrier)

---

### **Screening Value Block**

```
Problem: "Pengen tau kesehatan mental kamu, tapi malu pergi dokter?"
Outcome: "Coba test kesehatan mental, tau hasilnya langsung. Tanpa harus berkaitan dengan dokter."
Proof (on expand): "Test yang sama digunakan dokter, tapi kamu bisa lakukan sendiri di rumah."
CTA: "Lihat tes"
```

**Why it works**:
- Addresses cost + stigma: "tanpa dokter"
- Removes clinical framing: "tau hasilnya langsung"
- Builds trust: "test yang sama digunakan dokter"
- Scientific but accessible: No jargon

---

### **Social Proof / Testimonial**

```
Testimonial: "Aku kirain mental health itu cuma buat orang yang 'benar-benar sakit'. 
Ternyata semuanya bisa maintain kesehatan mental sejak dini. Thanks, Konsulin."
— Rini, 24, Content Creator
```

**Why it works**:
- Real voice: Conversational, grammatically human
- Relatable scenario: Young professional, creative field
- Destigmatizes: "Tidak hanya untuk yang 'sakit'"
- Authentic: Not overly polished

---

### **Use Case Narrative**

```
Scenario:
"Hari ini Aldi merasa tertekan, tapi enggak tau kenapa. Dia buka Konsulin, 
chat sama bot, cerita sedikit tentang harinya, dan langsung dapet insight. 
Ternyata, setiap kali deadline dekat, mood-nya turun.

Sekarang Aldi bisa plan lebih baik. Pas deadline, dia tetep calm."

CTA: "Seperti Aldi, mulai sekarang"
```

**Why it works**:
- Concrete scenario: Relatable situation
- Specific insight: "deadline dekat → mood turun" (actionable)
- Solution shown: "bisa plan lebih baik" (empowering)
- Call-to-action ties to story: "Seperti Aldi"

## Common Mistakes in Indonesian Copy

### ❌ **Mistake 1: Over-Translating English**

```
"Optimalisasi kesejahteraan psikologis Anda melalui intervensi digital berbasis AI"
```

**Problem**: Sounds corporate, clinical, not relatable.

```
✓ Better: "Pahami diri sendiri dengan Konsulin, kawan digital kamu"
```

---

### ❌ **Mistake 2: Using Formal Address (Anda)**

```
"Anda dapat menuliskan jurnal Anda"
```

**Problem**: Feels distant, corporate, not conversational.

```
✓ Better: "Kamu bisa tulis jurnal, tanpa ada yang lihat"
```

---

### ❌ **Mistake 3: Ignoring Stigma**

```
"Dapatkan akses ke layanan kesehatan mental profesional"
```

**Problem**: Doesn't address the fear that stops people from seeking help.

```
✓ Better: "Chat sama psikolog, percakapan kamu tetap pribadi"
```

---

### ❌ **Mistake 4: Making It Sound Too Easy**

```
"One tap away from mental wellness!"
```

**Problem**: Sounds gimmicky, unrealistic, loses trust.

```
✓ Better: "Mulai dari sini. Bukan instant healing, tapi bisa bantu kamu paham diri sendiri."
```

---

### ❌ **Mistake 5: Using Slang Inconsistently**

```
"Yo, ngecek mental health mu dengan AI yang keren abis!"
```

**Problem**: Mixing English slang + Indonesian feels forced, alienates non-Gen Z.

```
✓ Better: "Cek mental health kamu dengan test yang terbukti ilmiah"
```

## Copy Testing Checklist

Before publishing any section, ask:

- [ ] Can an Indonesian 18-year-old understand this without Googling?
- [ ] Does it address a specific fear or barrier (stigma, cost, time, privacy)?
- [ ] Does it feel like a friend talking, not a company selling?
- [ ] Is it problem-first, not feature-first?
- [ ] Does it avoid clinical language?
- [ ] Does it use "kamu" or direct address, not "pengguna"?
- [ ] Does it acknowledge cultural context (Indonesian mental health landscape)?
- [ ] Is the CTA clear and action-oriented?

If any answer is "no," revise the copy.

## Summary

**Golden Rule**: Write for someone texting a friend, not presenting to a conference. Use their language, address their fears, show you understand their context.

**Always ask**: "Would I say this to a friend in WhatsApp? If not, rewrite it."
