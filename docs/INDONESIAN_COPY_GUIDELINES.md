# Indonesian Copy Guidelines

konsulin.care is the home of #JadiTangguh, a citizen science movement on psychological resilience led by Konsulin. This guide covers tone and phrasing for Indonesian copy about mental health.

## Purpose

Help writers produce warm, honest copy that invites people to take part in a study.

## Site Map

Five main items: Beranda, Riset, FAQ, Tentang, Blog. Help, privacy, contact and services live in the footer.

## One Action

Taking part in a study. Every page points to this one action; do not write competing CTAs.

## Voice

- All visitor-facing copy is Indonesian.
- Address the reader as "Anda".
- Konsulin speaks as "saya" where a personal voice is needed: warm, conversational, not clinical.

## Tagline

Soften the #JadiTangguh tagline. Never promise outcomes, and never frame resilience as a duty or a cure.

"Tangguh" names the campaign only. Explain it as adapting, coping and seeking support. Never define it as strength, stoicism, or individual grit, and never frame resilience as solely an individual's responsibility.

## Honesty Rules

- No unconfirmed counts (participants, studies, results). Leave a visible `TODO(maintainer)` placeholder.
- Never state or imply ethics approval. Render the ethics status verbatim: "Persetujuan etik sedang dalam proses pengajuan."
- Screening is not diagnosis. Say so wherever screening appears.
- No invented testimonials, statistics or research claims.
- Privacy claims on the home page, the FAQ, the privacy page and `static/disclosure/protocol.md` must match. Never strengthen a claim without evidence.

## Safety Rule

Help resources stay reachable in the footer and wherever distress may be discussed. Never hide or bury them.

## Phrasing Principles

### Conversational, not clinical

| Clinical | Conversational |
|---|---|
| "Implementasikan protokol untuk mengelola distres" | "Ada cara-cara kecil yang bisa Anda coba" |
| "Optimalisasi kesejahteraan psikologis" | "Memahami diri sendiri, pelan-pelan" |

### Direct address

Use "Anda", not "pengguna" or "individu".

| Impersonal | Direct |
|---|---|
| "Partisipan dapat mengisi survei" | "Anda bisa mengisi survei ini kapan saja" |

### Question first

Start with what the reader may wonder, then explain.

- "Bagaimana orang Indonesia menghadapi masa sulit?"
- "Apa yang membuat sebagian orang bisa bangkit lagi?"

### Acknowledge stigma and privacy

Many people worry about judgment. Say plainly how data is handled and that taking part is voluntary. State only what is confirmed.

### Do not oversell

Avoid "instan", "pasti", "terbukti menyembuhkan". Prefer "mulai dari sini" and "bisa membantu Anda memahami".

## Terminology

| English | Indonesian |
|---|---|
| Mental health | Kesehatan mental |
| Resilience | Resiliensi psikologis |
| Screening | Skrining (bukan diagnosis) |
| Stress | Stres |
| Anxiety | Kecemasan |
| Depression | Depresi |
| Psychologist | Psikolog |
| Study / research | Studi / riset |
| Participant | Peserta |

### Avoid (feels pathologizing)

| Term | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "Gangguan mental" | Kesehatan mental, kondisi mental |
| "Pasien" | Anda, peserta |
| "Intervensi terapeutik" | Dukungan, bantuan |

## Phrasing Patterns

- Hook: "Pernah merasa...?" / "Bagaimana kalau...?"
- Outcome: "Anda bisa [aksi], [tanpa beban]" without promising results.
- Trust: "Dipimpin oleh Konsulin" and links to study details; no unconfirmed numbers.
- Action: "Mulai Survei (10 Menit)", "Pelajari riset ini".

### Sparse punctuation

Keep colons sparse in visitor-facing copy. Prefer short sentences that end with a full stop.

## Common Mistakes

- Over-translating English into stiff corporate Indonesian.
- Mixing slang inconsistently.
- Promising outcomes or framing resilience as a duty.
- Treating screening as diagnosis.
- Inventing counts or claims instead of using `TODO(maintainer)`.

## Copy Checklist

- [ ] Can an 18-year-old understand this without searching?
- [ ] Does it use "Anda" and, if personal, "saya"?
- [ ] Is it warm, not clinical or salesy?
- [ ] Does it avoid promises, counts and ethics claims not confirmed?
- [ ] Is screening described as not diagnosis, where relevant?
- [ ] Is there only one action, taking part in a study?
- [ ] Are help resources reachable?

## Summary

Write warmly and honestly, as a person would speak. If a fact is missing, leave a visible `TODO(maintainer)`.
