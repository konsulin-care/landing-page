# Data Use Agreement Template #JadiTangguh

This template is published so that an applicant can read the full terms before applying. Access to restricted data is granted only under an executed agreement, signed by the data steward, the second reviewer and the recipient's authorised representative. Requests are submitted to hello@konsulin.care

The agreement is a single instrument. Its confidentiality annex forms part of it, and no separate non-disclosure agreement is issued.

## 1. Parties and scope

- The provider is Konsulin, acting through the data steward and the second reviewer named in the data disclosure and handling protocol.
- The recipient is the institution named in the approved application, acting through the named researchers listed in that application.
- The agreement covers the restricted dataset version named in the annex. It does not extend to later versions, which require a new application.

## 2. Purpose limitation

- The recipient uses the data solely for the purpose and analysis plan stated in the approved application. Any extension of the purpose requires written approval before the work begins.
- The recipient does not use the data for any commercial purpose, for enforcement, for insurance assessment or for any decision about an identified individual.
- The recipient does not attempt to identify any participant, and does not claim that the data are anonymous under any jurisdiction.

## 3. Prohibited uses

- The recipient must not link public releases to restricted data, and must not publish results obtained by such a join. The public baseline covers the whole cohort, so linking it to the restricted panel would re-identify participants.
- The recipient must not share the data onward, in whole or in part, with any third party, including contractors and students not named in the application.
- The recipient must not combine the restricted data with other datasets for the purpose of re-identification.
- The recipient must not publish any cell count below 11, or any statistic from which such a count can be derived.

## 4. Security requirements

- The recipient stores the data on an access-controlled system within the recipient institution, encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Access is limited to the named researchers, individually authenticated, with no shared accounts.
- The recipient keeps an access log for the duration of the agreement and makes it available on request.

## 5. Incident reporting

- The recipient reports any suspected or actual breach, loss or unauthorised access to hello@konsulin.care within 72 hours of discovery.
- The report states what was disclosed, to whom, when, and the remedial action taken.
- The provider may suspend access while an incident is investigated.

## 6. Retention and destruction

- The recipient retains the data only for the period stated in the annex, and in any case no longer than the term of the agreement.
- On expiry the recipient destroys all copies, including backups and derived files, and confirms destruction in writing within 30 days.
- Destruction is certified by the recipient's authorised representative.

## 7. Publication review

- The recipient submits any manuscript, report or presentation using the data to the provider at least 30 days before submission or release.
- The provider reviews the output for disclosure risk only, and does not review scientific content.
- The recipient cites the dataset DOI and the protocol version in the data availability statement.

## 8. Audit rights

- The provider may audit the recipient's compliance once per term, on 30 days notice, and immediately after a reported incident.
- The audit covers storage arrangements, access logs, retention and destruction records, and onward-sharing arrangements.
- Findings are recorded, and a material finding ends the agreement.

## 9. Eligibility

- Applications are accepted from researchers affiliated with a recognised university, research institute, government agency or civil society organisation.
- A named researcher must hold a completed ethics review from their own institution where their institution requires one.
- Applications from individuals without an institutional affiliation are considered where a named institutional sponsor signs the agreement.

## 10. Assessment timeline

- The provider acknowledges an application within 5 working days.
- The steward and the second reviewer decide within 20 working days of a complete application.
- Where the application needs revision, the clock restarts when the revised application arrives.

## 11. Refusal grounds

- A refusal is issued where the purpose is outside the study's scope, where the applicant cannot demonstrate adequate security, where the applicant has previously breached an agreement, or where disclosure risk cannot be reduced to the documented level.
- Refusal grounds are recorded.

## 12. Appeal

- An applicant may appeal a refusal in writing within 20 working days of the decision.
- The appeal is decided by the steward and the second reviewer jointly, with written reasons.
- A further refusal is final for that application, and a materially different application may be submitted at any time.

## 13. Term, termination and contact

- The agreement runs for the term stated in the annex and may be terminated earlier by either party on 30 days written notice.
- The provider terminates immediately on a material breach, including a linking attempt or an onward share.
- Correspondence is addressed to hello@konsulin.care
